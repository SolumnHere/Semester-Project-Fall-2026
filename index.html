A New Icon Rises Above Saigon

On July 26, 2018, Landmark 81 officially welcomed its first visitors with the opening of Vincom Center Landmark 81. Standing 461.2 meters tall, the skyscraper introduced a new destination for shopping, dining, and entertainment, marking a significant milestone in the development of Ho Chi Minh City.

Reaching New Heights with SkyView

On April 28, 2019, Landmark 81 expanded its experiences with the opening of the SkyView observation deck and Vinpearl Luxury Landmark 81 hotel. Visitors could now admire panoramic views of the city while experiencing luxury hospitality high above the Saigon skyline.

A Partnership Beyond Borders

In June 2022, Vinpearl announced a strategic partnership with Marriott International. The agreement marked a new chapter for Landmark 81, bringing its luxury hotel into Marriott’s international hospitality network and introducing a new direction for the tower’s hospitality services.

Recognition Across Asia

Vinpearl Landmark 81, Autograph Collection received two prestigious hospitality awards at the World Travel Awards in 2023, recognizing its excellence as both a city hotel and a riverfront destination in Asia. These achievements strengthened its position in the international hospitality industry.

Continuing a Legacy of Excellence

Following its national recognition in 2024, Vinpearl Landmark 81 once again received Vietnam’s leading city hotel award in 2025. The achievement reflected its continued commitment to exceptional hospitality and memorable experiences in the heart of Ho Chi Minh City.

A Destination for Business and Luxury

FIn 2026, Vinpearl Landmark 81 earned national recognition for its business hospitality and presidential suite. These achievements marked another milestone in its evolution as a destination that combines modern architecture, luxury accommodation, and professional experiences.
